TLDR
The module

Each student first builds a brand for their own creative agency (A1). The aim is to understand what goes into building a brand and the decisions behind it, so students can read what a client wants to achieve. In A2, each student designs a digital brand experience for the client. In A3, groups design an integrated phygital brand experience for the same client, where an A2 digital experience can become one of its touchpoints. This semester's client is Mox Monster (founder: Kenneth), with a pop-up brief at Artbox featuring the "Unwanted" character series.

Weekly rhythm

A 2-hour OAL lesson (Rise, async on Brightspace) plus 8 hours of tutorial. Use roughly the first half for structured activities and the second half for consultation.

Three phases

Brand Foundation (Weeks 1–2) → Experience Design (Weeks 3–7) → Application & Integration (Weeks 8–17). Weeks 9–10 are the break.

Five things to know
  1. Tutorials open with a short refresher of that week's OAL lesson. The overlap with the OAL is intentional.
  2. Tutorial activities are done in groups so students learn from each other. Individual work is kept for the assignments.
  3. No new content runs while an assignment is due. Week 5 (A1-1) and Week 12 (A2) are consultation weeks.
  4. Each lesson and tutorial now names the brief it feeds. Look for the "Use this for A1-1 / A3" callouts and point students to them.
  5. Feedback follows three parts: a specific strength, one or two development areas framed as opportunities, then an overall direction. A1-2 feedback is 2–4 sentences; A1-1 and above is 4–8 paragraphs.

Teaching guide

What to emphasise, which brief each week feeds, and what to watch for.
Week 1 · Brand foundation (L01, L02, T01)
  • Emphasise: a brand is a system of meaning, not a logo. Students practise every A1-1 element on a shared "practice agency" before applying it to their own.
  • Feeds: A1-1, where students create their own creative agency brand.
  • Perceptual map: optional in A1-1. Students map against competitor types (e.g. "big network agency"), not researched named agencies, and it is marked as supporting evidence only. Teach that "an axis is a choice, not a compliment" and pair one "what we do" axis with one "who we suit" axis.
  • Terminology: use "Brand Essence" (as in L02 and the A1-1 brief), not "Brand Idea".
Week 2 · Identity and content (L03, L04, T02)
  • Emphasise: identity must express strategy. L04 covers IMC, content pillars and Message → Format → Platform. Launch planning has moved to L09.
  • Feeds: A1-1 (identity system and Brand Board). The moodboard is optional. A1-2 is a group Brand Board analysis of an existing brand.
  • Watch for: A1-2 requires a real-world visit. T02 ends with a Field Visit Plan so groups leave knowing where and when they will observe.
Week 3 · Journeys and UX flows (L05, L06)
  • Emphasise: journey maps with emotions, pain points and opportunities, then one key interaction moment turned into a UX flow.
  • Feeds: A2 (journey map, UX flow) and A1-1 refinement.
  • Milestone: A1-2 group presentations this week.
Week 4 · UI and Figma (L07)
  • Emphasise: visual hierarchy, colour and text styles, micro-interactions, and a first clickable prototype in Figma.
  • Watch for: this is students' first Figma session. Week 6 builds on it with advanced prototyping for A2.
Week 5 · A1-1 consult week
  • No new content. Run consultations and have students self-check against the rubric before the Sunday 2359 deadline.
Weeks 6–8 · A2 studio
  • Week 6: Mox Monster client briefing, then advanced Figma (components, variants, Smart Animate, overlays).
  • Weeks 7–8: scoping, journey refinement in the client context, and prototype development, with consultations.
  • Feeds: A2 (individual). The clickable prototype is the core deliverable: at least one complete user flow and 5–8 key screens. UX flow and prototype carry 55 of 100 marks.
  • Watch for: ideas that lack a real digital interaction. A2 requires a component that becomes a UX flow and prototype.
Weeks 9–10 · Break
Weeks 11–12 · A2 refinement and consult
  • Interaction testing and peer review in Week 11. Week 12 is consultations only, with A2 due Sunday 2359.
Week 13 · Phygital and A3 kick-off (L08)
  • Emphasise: designing beyond screens, and connecting physical and digital touchpoints.
  • Tutorial: A3 briefing and group formation. Groups compare members' A2 work and agree a shared direction.
Week 14 · Launch and spatial journey (L09, T06)
  • L09: plan entry touchpoints as Tease → Launch → Sustain, with one simple KPI per phase.
  • T06: students use the A3 stages: Entry → Discovery → Interaction → Extension → Exit. Groups with an A3 direction may use it; others use a practice scenario. Extension is the digital continuation beyond the space, distinct from Exit.
  • Feeds: the A3 Phygital Journey Map and Experience Demonstration.
Weeks 15–17 · A3 build and live presentation
  • Week 15: mid-check feedback on the system and prototype.
  • Week 16: activation planning and setup.
  • Week 17: live presentation with an Experience Demonstration. The brief's appendix frames it as Enter → Interact → Exit and keeps it to a simple, achievable scale. The demonstration carries 35 of 100 marks.
