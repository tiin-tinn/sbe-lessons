Synopsis

This module introduces students to the strategic, visual, and experiential dimensions of branding. Students begin by examining how brands build meaning, equity, and differentiation through positioning, story, and identity systems. The module then extends into content strategy, customer journey mapping, UX/UI thinking, and spatial or phygital experience design, showing how brand strategy is translated into coherent audience experiences across touchpoints.

Module Learning Outcomes

On successful completion of the module, students will be able to:

  • Analyse and formulate a coherent brand foundation system that defines positioning, story essence, identity, and content direction.
  • Design customer-centred brand experiences through journey mapping, UX/UI thinking, and phygital touchpoint planning.
  • Develop and present an integrated brand experience and launch proposal in response to a live client brief.

The signature pedagogies used in this module are: Project-based Learning, Direct Instruction. Assignments will mimic industry projects to provide students with a realistic work scenario. Students are expected to work individually or in groups.
