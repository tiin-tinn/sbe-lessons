AGENCY BRAND FOUNDATION SYSTEM

WEIGHTAGE : 25%
Background

Brands are not just visual identities. They are systems of meaning, built through strategy, positioning, and consistent expression across touchpoints.

In weeks 1–2, you will explore:

  • What brands represent beyond logos
  • How brand equity is built over time
  • How brands define their position in the market
  • How identity systems express brand belief and personality

You will also complete a Field Assignment (A1-2), in which you analyse how an existing brand operates in a real-world context. Apply what you learn from it to your own agency.

This assignment marks a shift from: Analysing brands → to becoming one

You are now required to develop your own creative agency brand, which will serve as your identity throughout the rest of the module.
Objective

Develop a clear and coherent Brand Foundation System for your own creative agency.

This system should clearly define:

  • What your agency stands for
  • Who your agency is for (your ideal clients)
  • How your agency is positioned
Task

Create a designed Brand Foundation System for your creative agency.
Important Notes
  • This is a strategy-led design task, not purely aesthetic brand creation
  • Your work should demonstrate a clear connection between brand strategy and visual expression, not just aesthetic choices.
Submission

Submission Format

You are required to submit a designed Brand Foundation document.
* Your document should allow someone to understand your brand clearly within 1–2 minutes of viewing.

This document should present your agency brand as a coherent and structured system, combining strategy, identity, and rationale into a single, well-designed output.

Each page should communicate a clear idea and be easily understood within a few seconds. Your work will be assessed on how clearly your brand can be understood, not how visually complex it is.

Format Requirements

  • Canvas size: A4 Landscape/Portraits (297mm x 210mm or 210mm x 297mm)
  • Recommended length: 5-6 pages (maximum 8 pages)
  • Export and submit as a single PDF
  • Submit via Brightspace

You may organise your pages to clearly reflect the sections (Brand Strategy, Identity System, Brand Board, Rationale).

This is not a slide deck - each page must be intentionally designed with clear layout, hierarchy, and visual structure

Structure (Recommended)
Your document should be organised into the following sections:

1. Brand Strategy
Define the core foundation of your agency:

  • Agency Name and Name Story: what your name means, and 6–8 words or images it suggests. Use these words throughout your document.
  • Agency Description: what your agency does
  • Target Audience: your ideal client, the type of business your agency is built for and the problem you solve for them (by sector, stage or values). This does not mean you turn other clients away. “All brands” is not accepted.
  • Brand Purpose: your Why, in one sentence starting “We believe…”
  • Brand Positioning: For [ideal client] who [need], [agency] is the [category] that [point of difference] because [reason to believe].
  • Brand Idea: the single idea that ties your strategy together.
  • Brand Personality: a primary archetype (and one optional secondary archetype) with 2–3 personality traits.
  • Optional: Perceptual Map. You may include a perceptual map showing where your agency sits against three types of agency or service your ideal clients could choose instead (for example, a large full-service agency, a low-cost template service or a freelance designer). You do not need to research named agencies. Each axis should be a real choice that an agency could proudly make, and your agency’s position should match the point of difference in your positioning statement.

2. Identity System
Develop a consistent visual and verbal system. Each element should be intentionally selected and clearly linked to your brand personality and positioning:

  • Logo system: primary logo and icon version
  • Colour Palette: roles (primary, secondary, accent, neutral) and HEX codes
  • Typography
  • Imagery Style
  • Graphical Elements
  • Tone of Voice

Your system should clearly reflect your brand personality and positioning.

3. Brand Board (System Summary)
Include a 1-page Brand Board that:

  • Summarises your identity system
  • Demonstrates consistency across elements
  • Communicates your agency at a glance

4. Rationale
Provide a concise explanation that clearly links your strategy to your visual decisions:

  • Why your design decisions were made
  • How your strategy informs your identity
  • How your system works as a whole

Important Notes

  • This is not a slide deck
  • Each page must be intentionally designed with:
    • clear layout
    • strong visual hierarchy
    • consistent use of your identity system
  • Your document should function as a mini brand guide, not separate slides placed together
  • If your visual choices cannot be explained, they are considered decorative
The goals of this assignment are to help you
  • Apply branding principles to create a coherent system
  • Translate strategy into visual and verbal expression
  • Develop a clear and differentiated brand
  • Understand how identity supports communication and positioning
