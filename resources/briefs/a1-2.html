TYPE : GROUP SUBMISSION : WEEK 03
FIELD ASSIGNMENT (FA):
BRAND BOARD ANALYSIS

WEIGHTAGE : 5%
Background

You are part of a creative agency tasked with understanding how brands communicate their identity through visual and verbal systems.

Before developing your own brand, your Creative Director has asked your team to study an existing brand and analyse how its belief, personality, and positioning are expressed through design.

Strong brand identities are not random — they are carefully constructed systems where every element (colour, typography, imagery, tone) reflects a deeper strategic intent.

This assignment focuses on observing, analysing, and translating an existing brand into a structured Brand Board.
Objective

Analyse an existing brand and translate its identity into a clear and coherent Brand Board.
Task

Step 1: Choose a Brand

Select one existing brand.

Examples:

  • Lifestyle (Aesop, Nike, Muji)
  • F&B (Starbucks, Toast Box, Shake Shack)
  • Service spaces (Clinics, Gyms, Salons, Cafés)

! Important Requirement

You must visit and observe the brand in a real-world setting.

Your analysis should be based on:

  • Physical environment (space, layout, materials)
  • Brand touchpoints (signage, packaging, menus, interfaces)
  • Customer interaction (how people engage with the space or service)

Online references (e.g. website, social media) can be used only to support your observation, not replace it.

Step 2: Study the Brand

Observe how the brand expresses itself across touchpoints:

  • Website / App
  • Social media
  • Physical space (if applicable)
  • Packaging / advertisements

Step 3: Create a Brand Board

Translate your observations into a Brand Board.

Your Brand Board must include:

1. Brand Overview

  • Brand Name
  • One-line description of what the brand stands for

2. Logo System

  • Main logo
  • Variations (if applicable)

3. Colour Palette

  • 3–5 key colours
  • Indicate usage (primary / secondary / accent)

4. Typography

  • 1–2 type styles used
  • Indicate purpose (headers, body, etc.)

5. Visual / Imagery Direction

  • 3–6 images reflecting brand tone
  • Include real references from the brand

6. Tone of Voice (Short)

  • 3 keywords describing how the brand communicates

(e.g. playful, calm, bold, minimal)

7. Rationale (Very Important)

Provide a short explanation (150–200 words):
How do the visual and verbal elements reflect the brand’s personality, positioning, and intended experience?
Important Notes
  • This is an analysis and translation task, not brand creation
  • Focus on observing patterns and consistency, not copying randomly
  • Your board should reflect:
  • What the brand consistently does
  • Not isolated examples
Submission

Your group is required to present your Brand Board Analysis in Week 03.

Submission requirements:

  • Prepare a slide deck (PowerPoint / Canva / Google Slides)
  • Slides must clearly present your Brand Board and analysis
  • Submit your slides (PDF format) via Brightspace before your presentation

Each group will deliver a presentation of their Brand Board Analysis.

Presentation guidelines:

  • Duration: 8–10 minutes per group
  • Clearly explain:
  • The chosen brand and context
  • Key observations from field visit
  • Brand Board elements (logo, colour, type, imagery, tone)
  • Rationale (most important)
  • Slides should support explanation, not just display content
  • All group members should participate in presenting

Field Observation Requirement (Compulsory)

You must include a minimum of 3 original photos taken during your site visit.

These photos should document:

  • The physical environment (e.g. store layout, signage, materials)
  • Key brand touchpoints (e.g. packaging, menus, displays, interfaces)
  • Customer interaction or experience moments (where possible)
  • Each photo must be included in your slides
  • Include a short caption explaining:
  • What you observed
  • Why it is relevant to the brand’s identity

Photos must be clear, relevant, and taken by your team. Screenshots from the internet will not be accepted as substitutes.
The goals of this assignment are to help you
  • Understand how brands express identity through design systems
  • Develop observation and analysis skills
  • Practice translating real-world brand elements into structured outputs
  • Build confidence before developing your own brand (A1)
