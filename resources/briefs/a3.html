Client

Heritage Bay (HB)

Background

In Assignment 1, you explored how branding shapes perception through positioning, identity, and visual expression.

In Assignment 2, you developed an individual Brand Experience proposal for Heritage Bay by identifying a meaningful experience opportunity and translating it into a customer journey, digital interaction, and interactive prototype.

In professional practice, experience design is rarely developed in isolation. Design teams evaluate multiple opportunities, exercise design judgement, and collaboratively determine which direction offers the greatest value for both the client and its users.

For this final assignment, your group will review the opportunities explored in Assignment 2 and determine the strongest direction for further development. You may refine an existing concept, expand an existing proposal, combine complementary ideas, or evolve a new direction informed by your group’s collective exploration.

Your task is to develop a stronger, more complete, and more tangible brand experience for Heritage Bay by demonstrating how the proposed experience works across relevant physical and digital touchpoints, and how it could be experienced in an authentic environment.

Objective

Develop a stronger and more complete brand experience for Heritage Bay by evaluating opportunities identified in Assignment 2 and collaboratively refining them into a cohesive proposal.

You are required to:

  • Evaluate the opportunities explored in Assignment 2 and determine the strongest direction for further development.
  • Develop a holistic brand experience that considers relevant physical and digital touchpoints.
  • Apply branding, customer experience, and interaction design principles to create a coherent and meaningful experience.
  • Demonstrate how users discover, access, interact with, and continue engaging with the proposed experience.
  • Communicate and justify your proposal through appropriate design artefacts, interactive prototypes, and experience demonstrations.
Task

Working in your group, review the opportunities explored in Assignment 2 and determine the strongest direction for further development.

Your group should evaluate the strengths and potential of the ideas explored by each member and exercise design judgement to develop a proposal that delivers greater value for both Heritage Bay and its users.

The final proposal may:

  • Refine and further develop an existing Assignment 2 concept.
  • Expand an existing concept into a more complete brand experience.
  • Combine complementary concepts where appropriate.
  • Evolve a new direction informed by the group’s collective exploration and insights from Assignment 2.

The selected direction should be informed by the group’s evaluation of the opportunities explored in Assignment 2 and supported by clear design justification.

Building upon the interactive prototype developed in Assignment 2, further refine and expand the digital interaction to support the group’s final proposal, while demonstrating how relevant physical and digital touchpoints work together to create a more complete Heritage Bay brand experience.

Assessment Requirements

Your group will present a proposal demonstrating how the selected experience has been refined and further developed from Assignment 2.

The presentation should communicate the complete experience through the following assessment components. Each component should build upon and support the others to communicate one cohesive and well-justified brand experience for Heritage Bay.

APPENDIX A — EXPERIENCE DEMONSTRATION GUIDELINES

What is the Experience Demonstration?

The Experience Demonstration is an opportunity to communicate how your proposed experience could exist in a real-world context.

Rather than presenting only slides or a prototype, your group should create a simple demonstration that brings together the key touchpoints of your proposal into one cohesive experience.

Your Goal

Help the audience:

  • Understand your proposal
  • Experience the key interaction
  • Appreciate how the physical and digital touchpoints work together

Minimum Requirements

Your Experience Demonstration should communicate how the proposed experience could exist in a real-world context. At a minimum, it should include the following:

1. Entry Experience

Demonstrate how users first encounter and enter your proposed experience.

Examples may include:

  • Signage
  • QR codes
  • Posters
  • Digital invitations
  • Entry prompts

2. Core Experience

Demonstrate the key interaction within your proposed experience.

Your demonstration should allow the audience to:

  • Experience the core interaction
  • Navigate the interactive prototype
  • Understand the value of the proposed experience

3. Interactive Prototype

Present the refined digital interaction developed from Assignment 2.

Requirements:

  • Clickable Figma prototype
  • Demonstrated using an appropriate digital device (mobile phone, tablet, laptop, or external display)

4. Supporting Touchpoints

Demonstrate how relevant physical and digital touchpoints support the proposed experience.

Examples may include:

  • Posters or signage
  • Printed mockups
  • QR codes
  • Product tags
  • Environmental graphics
  • Supporting props or presentation materials

The emphasis is on communicating the proposed experience clearly rather than producing a large or complex installation.

Scale Expectation (Important)

The Experience Demonstration should be achievable within the available venue environment.

The emphasis is on communicating your proposed experience clearly rather than producing a large or complex installation. Simple mockups, printed materials, digital devices, environmental graphics, and supporting props are acceptable where they effectively communicate your proposal.

A well-considered and coherent demonstration is more valuable than a complex setup that does not meaningfully support the proposed experience.

Avoid demonstrations that:

  • Rely only on presentation slides without allowing the audience to experience the proposal.
  • Present the prototype without showing how it connects to the wider brand experience.
  • Include static displays that provide little or no interaction.
  • Introduce unnecessary complexity that distracts from the proposed experience.

Framework

Enter
How do users discover or enter your experience?
↓
Interact
What do users experience or interact with?
↓
Exit
What do users leave with, learn, or do next?

A successful Experience Demonstration allows the audience to clearly understand, experience, and appreciate your proposed brand experience within the practical constraints of the assessment environment.
