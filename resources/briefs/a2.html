2STBRAND - Strategic Brand Engagement Official Open
A2 BRIEF
TYPE : INDIVIDUAL SUBMISSION : WEEK 11
BRAND EXPERIENCE DESIGN

WEIGHTAGE : 35%

Client
Heritage Bay (HB)
Background

In Assignment 1, you developed a Brand Foundation System by creating your own creative agency, defining its positioning, belief, and identity.

Brands, however, are not only defined by how they look — they are shaped by how they are experienced.

Your agency has now been approached by a real client, [Client], who is looking to strengthen their brand through a more meaningful and engaging experience.

[Client] is seeking to:

  • Improve how customers experience and engage with the brand across relevant touchpoints
  • Create a more coherent journey across touchpoints
  • Develop experiences that reflect their brand identity and values

This assignment shifts your role from: Defining a brand → Designing how that brand is experienced

You will now take on the role of a design agency, applying your perspective to design a structured and intentional brand experience for a real client.

Objective

Design a Brand Experience System that translates brand strategy into a clear and usable interaction.

You are required to:

  • Understand how users may discover, engage with, and experience the brand
  • Map a proposed customer experience journey for your selected opportunity
  • Identify key interaction moments and opportunities for engagement
  • Design a meaningful and coherent experience across digital, physical, or phygital touchpoints
  • Translate brand strategy into experience design
Task

Using your agency brand from Assignment 1, develop a Brand Experience Design for [Client].

Your proposal should focus on designing a meaningful and coherent brand experience that improves how users discover, engage with, or interact with the brand.

You are not required to redesign [Client’s] entire website. Instead, identify and propose a focused brand experience opportunity that could strengthen how users interact with and experience the [Client] brand.

Examples may include:

  • Discovery experiences
  • Workshop or event booking journeys
  • Cultural storytelling interaction
  • Gifting or product recommendation experience
  • Onboarding experience for first-time visitors
  • Retail browsing or exploration journey
  • Community engagement or loyalty experience
  • Post-purchase engagement experience
Submission

Your submission should include:

A. Customer Journey Map
Create a journey map that includes:

  • Persona
  • Key phases of the customer journey
  • User actions and interactions
  • Thoughts and emotions
  • Pain points and opportunities

B. Experience Concept
Define your proposed experience:

  • What focused brand experience or interaction are you designing for?
  • What problem or opportunity are you addressing?
  • How does this align with Heritage Bay’s brand?
  • How does your agency’s perspective influence this approach?

C. UX Flow
Develop a UX flow that shows:

  • Step-by-step interaction process
  • Key decision points
  • Logical structure of the experience

D. Prototype & Interaction Design (CORE DELIVERABLE)
Translate your UX flow into a clickable prototype.

Students may propose the most appropriate interface format for their experience (e.g. mobile, desktop, tablet, kiosk, or hybrid interfaces), depending on the nature of their proposed interaction and user context.

The selected interface format should be appropriate to the intended experience, interaction context, and user journey.

The prototype should be developed at a medium- to high-fidelity level, with sufficient visual and interaction detail to clearly communicate the intended user experience.

You are not expected to produce production-ready interfaces, but the prototype should demonstrate thoughtful interaction, usability, visual coherence, and clear application of UX/UI principles.

Your prototype must:

  • Be created using Figma
  • Demonstrate a clear and complete interaction flow
  • Include key screens required to understand the experience
  • Show navigation, transitions, feedback, and application of Don Norman design principles

Minimum Requirement:

  • At least 1 complete user flow
  • At least 5–8 key screens
  • Clickable navigation between screens

E. Physical / Phygital Extension (Optional)
Where relevant, you may include:

  • Physical environment or spatial interaction
  • Integration between physical and digital touchpoints

This section is optional and should support your core digital experience, not replace it.

F. Rationale
Provide a clear explanation of:

  • Your design decisions
  • How the experience reflects the brand
  • How your agency’s identity informs the solution

Submission

  • Create your work using Figma
  • Export and submit as a PDF
  • Include a link to your prototype
  • Submit both via Brightspace
The goals of this assignment are to help you
  • Translate brand strategy into clear, meaningful, and usable brand experience
  • Apply CX, UX, and UI principles in a structured way
  • Design coherent and usable interaction flows
  • Develop working prototypes that demonstrate interaction thinking

Your solution should focus on a clear digital experience. You may explore how this could extend into physical touchpoints in future assignments.
