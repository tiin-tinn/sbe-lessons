A2 / EXAMPLE 05 · BRAND EXPERIENCE DESIGN

Trace Your Craft: Product Stories.

A redesigned product page where a “Begin the Journey” button reveals the story behind a piece — its motif, craft process and artisans — with a community to join after purchase.

01 / OVERVIEW

The thinking.

From the problem they found to the experience they designed.

01 / THE GAP

The challenge

Heritage Bay’s products look good online, but each product page says little about what makes that piece meaningful.

Batik information sits in the About tab, first-time visitors must dig through collections to find products, and chances to join a community are limited.

02 / THE IDEA

The proposal

The homepage showcases featured products with short collection descriptions. On the product page, a “Begin the Journey” button sits above Add to cart.

The journey has four stages — Design meaning, Craft processes, Meet the artisans and Final product — tracked with a progress bar. After purchase, customers can join the Heritage Bay community.

03 / THE VALUE

Why it works

  • Placing the journey above Add to cart makes the story part of the buying decision.
  • Tooltips and hover cards replace blocks of text, so users explore rather than read.
  • Keeping Heritage Bay’s black and white palette, with grey and blue accents, stays true to the brand.
02 / JOURNEY

How it unfolds.

The user’s path through the experience.

  1. 01HomepageSee featured products and what each collection is about.
  2. 02Product pageOpen the Kotak Melati baggy jeans and tap Begin the Journey.
  3. 03Design meaningTap parts of the jeans to reveal what the motif means.
  4. 04Craft processesHover over each production stage to see how and when it was made.
  5. 05Meet the artisansDiscover the people behind the piece.
  6. 06Buy and belongPurchase, then join the Heritage Bay community.
