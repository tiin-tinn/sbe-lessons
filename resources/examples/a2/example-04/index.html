Find Your Batik.

A mobile experience that helps young shoppers picture themselves in batik: browse looks by style, then try pieces on in an AI fitting room.

01 / OVERVIEW

The thinking.

From the problem they found to the experience they designed.

01 / THE GAP

The challenge

Heritage Bay’s website works as a product showcase rather than an experience. It’s hard to picture how pieces will look, and the size guide feels intimidating.

The brand has no permanent store, and batik is often seen as something only older people wear.

02 / THE IDEA

The proposal

Find Your Batik mirrors a physical store visit. Choosing a style category is like walking to a section, browsing the lookbook is like scanning racks and mannequins, and checkout is the cashier.

The highlight is the AI Fitting Room: users upload a full-body photo and receive a generated image of themselves wearing the piece.

03 / THE VALUE

Why it works

  • Seeing themselves in the clothes shows young customers that batik suits them too.
  • It brings a fitting-room moment online for a brand without a permanent store.
  • The idea draws on the student’s own experience of an AI-powered museum exhibit.
02 / JOURNEY

How it unfolds.

The user’s path through the experience.

  1. 01Choose a styleTrendy & Streetwear, Smart & Casual or Classic & Timeless.
  2. 02Browse looksScroll a Pinterest-style board of styled photos.
  3. 03Pick a pieceOpen a product from the look.
  4. 04Try it onUpload a photo and let the AI Fitting Room generate the look.
  5. 05Choose a sizeUse the size finder, then add to cart.
  6. 06PayCheck out with a card or digital wallet.

FOR LEARNERS

What to look for.

  1. How each digital step is mapped to a familiar moment in a physical store.
  2. An insight drawn from personal experience, used to justify the AI feature.
  3. Small UI decisions, such as rounded corners and tighter letter spacing, explained in the rationale.
